1.2mm Microfiber PU Leather for Bags (Wholesale)

Our 1.2mm microfiber PU leather is engineered for handbag, backpack, and small leather goods producers who need a tear-resistant skin that still skives, folds, and edges like a premium hide. As a factory supplying microfiber PU leather wholesale to bag makers across Europe, North America, and Southeast Asia, we run this base cloth at 54-58" (137-147 cm) width on continuous rolls so your cutting tables waste less between nests. This page lays out the exact specs, certifications, and ordering terms our bag clients ask for before they place a sampling order.

Microfiber synthetic leather differs from ordinary PU on a knit or woven backing: the base is a needle-punched non-woven of ultra-fine polyamide filaments impregnated with polyurethane, which is why it carries a leather-like grain, holds stitch tension, and resists delamination far better than coated fabric. For bags that get yanked, stuffed, and dropped, that structural difference is the whole point.

Why Choose Microfiber Over Standard PU for Bags?

Bag makers come to microfiber PU leather wholesale supply when ordinary coated PU starts failing in the field, usually at corners, handle roots, and zipper tape. Here is how the two compare on the points that drive returns:

  • Stitch tear-out: the non-woven base anchors thread far better than a 2-3 ply knit backing, so handle attachments and gusset seams hold under load instead of zippering open.
  • Delamination: microfiber is impregnated, not laminated, so there is no skin-to-fabric glue line to peel; this is why peel values sit at 28-40 N/25mm rather than the 10-18 N/25mm typical of cheap coated PU.
  • Grain memory: embossed grains stay crisp after folding and creasing, which matters for structured totes and flap bags.
  • Hand and weight: at 620-740 g/m² it feels substantial without the rubbery stiffness of PVC, and it skives cleanly for turned edges.
  • Abrasion: 40,000-100,000 Martindale cycles keeps the grain intact through years of daily carry, versus the visible scuffing standard PU shows by 15,000-20,000.
